# RemiLink Website Rebuild — Project Context

## What This Is

RemiLink (remilink.com) is an AI outsourcing/implementation company. This repo (`fearless-lark.github.io`) hosts the company website on GitHub Pages. We are doing a full website rebuild and strategic repositioning — from a generic "we do everything" AI shop to a focused, trust-led B2B service with clear specialization.

## Key Decisions & Corrections

- **MVP phase duration**: 3–5 months (not 3–4.5)
- **No adtech**: The domain is EdTech. "Adtech" in recordings is a speech error.
- **Form submissions**: Go to info@remilink.com via formsubmit.co (keeps email hidden + DDoS protection). No replacement needed.
- **Analytics**: Post-launch item, not a launch blocker
- **Outreach tools**: Clay, Apollo, LinkedIn Helper are current vision — other tools may be explored
- **Team presentation**: About page launches founder-first; team section shows roles, not faces (no team photos confirmed)
- **Frontend**: "We don't write frontend. Well, we can, but it's not what we offer."
- **Computer Vision**: Capability exists, deliberately shelved (niche market)
- **Agentic POC**: Aspirational offering — system doesn't fully exist yet
- **Site metrics killed**: "100+ AI initiatives," "4.9/5 rating," "Top 0.001%" — all dropped, unsourceable
- **Service labels (public)**: AI Strategy Session → Discovery & Blueprint → Concept Validation → Production-Ready Build → Scale & Optimize
- **CTA**: "Discuss Your AI Project" + "Get an honest assessment of feasibility, timeline, and cost — directly from our chief architect."
- **Pricing display**: Entry costs + monthly rates on site. No totals. Durations shown separately from prices.
- **Starting monthly rate**: $15,000–18,000/month (not $25K as originally estimated)
- **Secondary CTA (PDF)**: Deferred to post-launch
