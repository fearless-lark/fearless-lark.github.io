## Current Working Truth

- Remilink’s strongest current commercial story is founder-led, validation-first AI implementation.
- Primary commercial focus is e-commerce shopping assistants / complex search and related AI discovery work.
- Secondary focus is security-sensitive in-house AI enablement.
- EdTech remains a real area of interest, but proof and packaging are thinner and should be treated cautiously.
- Primary website action is contact-form submission routed to `info@remilink.com`, with manual founder/architect follow-up within 24 hours.
- No default calendar embed, no autoresponder-style contact flow, no immediate CRM requirement, and analytics should be added at launch or immediately after.
- Clay, Apollo, and LinkedIn Helper are current tooling ideas, not commitments.
- `MVP = 3–5 months` in the current documented delivery model.
- AI-native messaging should explicitly include data-engineering best practices, client-aligned tool/model selection, and the wider team expertise behind small staffed engagements.
- Brand tone should stay helpful and low-pressure; the founder explicitly associates `Remi` with a “friend / helper” undertone.

## Current Risks and Gaps

- Proof claims and metrics still need sourcing.
- The exact company-size target is still too broad.
- The post-first-contact sales pipeline is underdefined.
- The team needs founder and team photography for credible site presentation.
- Public language around pricing, timelines, and comparative claims still needs careful restraint.

## Next Recommended Work

1. Audit every proof claim and every case study for sourceability and NDA-safe expansion.
2. Decide the exact website service labels, industry framing, and CTA button copy.
3. Gather founder/team imagery and finalize the contact-flow implementation path.
4. Refresh the website build backlog so it starts from the new founder-input truth instead of older assumptions.
5. Start website implementation only after the above inputs are stable enough to avoid rework.
