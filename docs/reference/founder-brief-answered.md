## 1. Who Are Your Buyers? ✅ Answered

> *Describe 2–3 most typical client types.*

### Client Type 1: E-Commerce Companies Seeking AI Search/Assistants

- **Title**: CTO, VP of Engineering, Engineering Manager, Product Owner, Product Manager, CEO/Founder
- **Company size**: 100–2,000 employees. Specialized online stores with complex product catalogs (thousands/tens of thousands of products, many nested categories). Ideal example: large auto parts store.
- **Industry**: E-commerce — auto parts, clothing, cosmetics, building materials
- **Trigger**: Competitors have already tried or implemented AI solutions; they want to stay competitive
- **Fears**: Spending budget on a prototype that never reaches production; picking the wrong partner; long timelines scaring stakeholders
- **What would convince them to start a conversation**: Seeing specific, relevant case studies; a clear, transparent methodology; a free 2-hour strategy call with no commitment
- **Where do they come from**: Primarily LinkedIn
- **Vendor selection process**: Not explicitly described. Likely committee-based for larger companies.

### Client Type 2: Companies Needing In-House AI / On-Prem Solutions

- **Title**: CTO, CEO/Founder
- **Company size**: 50–1,000+ employees
- **Industry**: Financial services, healthcare, insurance, or any company with strict data policies
- **Trigger**: Corporate data security policy that prohibits using external AI vendors
- **Fears**: Data leaving the organization; compliance violations; vendor lock-in
- **What would convince them**: Demonstration that all development stays on local infrastructure; security-first approach
- **Where do they come from**: LinkedIn, possibly referrals

### Client Type 3: Companies New to AI (AI Audit Candidates)

- **Title**: CTO, Product Owner, CEO/Founder — broadest ICP
- **Company size**: Varies
- **Industry**: Any business that hasn't tried AI yet, doesn't know where to start
- **Trigger**: Wanting to explore AI but not knowing whom to ask or what's feasible
- **Fears**: Getting sold something they don't need; wasting budget on the wrong approach
- **What would convince them**: Free 2-hour strategy session; transparent assessment of what's feasible and what's not

### Psychographic Profile (All Types)
- "Adequate" clients who understand reality
- People who may have already been somewhere, tried something
- Not people who fall for empty promises
- "I would want to work with those who understand this"

## 3. Your Services — Structured and Honest ✅ Answered

### Service 1: AI Development (Discovery & Blueprint → Concept Validation → Production-Ready Build → Scale & Optimize)

| | Discovery & Blueprint | Concept Validation | Production-Ready Build | Scale & Optimize |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **What you do** | Meet stakeholders, gather requirements, create technical blueprint | Validate AI concept with real data, no heavy integrations | Integrate into main app, deploy, prepare for production | Scale to all users/products, monitoring, guardrails, optimization |
| **Deliverables** | VBS, VBO, PRD, Technical Blueprint, Commercial Proposal | Working demo with real data | Solution for subset of users/products | Full production system |
| **Duration** | 1–3 weeks | 2–4 months | 3–5 months | 4–7 months |
| **Cost** | $5,000–$10,000 | ~$15,000–18,000/month | ~$15,000–18,000+/month | Varies |
| **Team** | Architect, 1 dev, 1 BA | Architect (PT), 2 devs, 1 BA, 1 PM (PT) | Same + tester | Growing team |
| **Who it's for** | Any company with an AI idea | Companies that need concept validation | Validated concepts ready for integration | Proven solutions ready for scale |
| **How it's different** | Artifacts are portable — client can take them anywhere | No unnecessary spending — prove one category, not ten | Same team from Discovery — no handoff, no lost context | Full lifecycle ownership |
| **What's not included** | Implementation | Production-grade engineering, integrations | Full scaling, monitoring | — |

**Cross-cutting differentiators** (apply to all services):
- **Data engineering best practices**: Every AI solution is built on a proper data engineering foundation — not an afterthought
- **Enterprise tooling flexibility**: Always align the exact toolset and AI models with the client, including fully on-prem instruments where required
- **Team expertise at individual prices**: Even on 1–2–3 person projects, the client gets access to the experience and expertise of the entire team — engineers and architects specializing across all AI disciplines

### Service 2: AI Shopping Assistants for E-Commerce
- Intelligent search agents for complex product catalogs
- Consider structured and unstructured data (descriptions, reviews)
- Sales, consulting, and recommendation capabilities

### Service 3: In-House AI Systems / MLOps
- Setting up AI on client's own infrastructure
- Task analysis, capacity estimation, cost assessment, procurement assistance
- All data stays in-house
- Enterprise tooling flexibility: always align the exact toolset and models with the client, including on-prem instruments

### Service 4: AI Audit / Strategic Workshop
- 2-hour free session OR full week ($5,000)
- Review business ideas with stakeholders; examine technical stack with tech team
- Deliverable: feasibility assessment with timelines, costs, quality expectations
- Identify what's NOT feasible and what data needs to be collected
- Surface new opportunities the client hasn't considered
