# Offers and ICP

## Current Offer Stack

### AI Audit and Discovery

- `Confirmed`: Remilink can offer a low-friction AI audit / strategic workshop to assess what is feasible, what is not, and what data, architecture, or scope work is missing.
- `Confirmed`: Discovery is also the first stage of larger build engagements and produces portable artifacts the client can reuse elsewhere.
- `Inferred`: This is the clearest entry product because it matches the founder’s strengths in diagnosis and protects the client from premature build commitments.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### Validation-First AI Builds

- `Confirmed`: The most developed concrete use case is AI shopping assistants / intelligent product search for complex e-commerce catalogs.
- `Confirmed`: The founder describes POC work as the place to prove the concept on one representative slice before heavy engineering or broad rollout.
- `Confirmed`: The envisioned shopping-assistant flow should use not only structured product data, but also descriptions and user comments, and it should help consult, recommend, and sell rather than just match keywords.
- `Inferred`: This validation-first build story is more credible than a generic “we build any AI app” service line.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### Secure In-House AI Systems

- `Confirmed`: Remilink is interested in helping companies deploy AI capabilities on their own infrastructure when data security policy makes external vendors hard to use.
- `Confirmed`: This includes estimating required compute, planning deployment, and helping keep models, data, and code on-prem or otherwise in-house.
- `Inferred`: This is best framed as a security-sensitive operating context rather than as a narrow industry offer.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

### MVP and Production Delivery

- `Confirmed`: Remilink does not want to stop at strategy or prototype work; MVP and Production phases are part of the company’s intended model.
- `Confirmed`: The founder emphasizes that these later phases introduce real integration, deployment, guardrails, monitoring, and scaling work.
- `Inferred`: The company should present itself as full-lifecycle, but only after explaining why the early validation stages exist.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Priority Markets

### E-Commerce

- `Confirmed`: E-commerce is the clearest current commercial focus.
- `Confirmed`: The best-defined ICP is specialized online stores with large, complicated catalogs where navigation and search are real user pain points.
- `Confirmed`: Trigger events include competitors already trying similar solutions and frustration with primitive site search.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### EdTech

- `Confirmed`: The company is not working with AdTech. References in the transcript that sound like “adtech” are about EdTech.
- `Confirmed`: EdTech remains a secondary or exploratory direction with thinner proof and less founder confidence.
- `Confirmed`: The only concrete EdTech-adjacent work mentioned so far is a CRM/product build for an online school, and it was not primarily an AI project.
- `Confirmed`: Custom CRM development for education buyers does not currently look like a strong Remilink offer because smaller schools may lack budget, larger organizations likely already have systems, and the AI wedge is weak.
- `Exploratory`: More compelling EdTech ideas would be tutoring agents, RAG over learning materials, or similar AI-native experiences, but these are still hypothesis-level.
- `Open`: The specific EdTech ICP, offer packaging, and proof story are not strong enough yet for aggressive website specialization.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### Data-Sensitive Organizations

- `Confirmed`: Finance, healthcare, insurance, and similar organizations appear in the notes mostly as examples of companies with strong data-security requirements.
- `Inferred`: The current viable story here is not “we dominate these industries,” but “we can help security-sensitive teams adopt AI responsibly and in-house.”
- `Open`: Whether this should become a formal site vertical or remain a secondary context page is still unresolved.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Buyer Triggers and Fears

### Trigger Events

- `Confirmed`: Competitors have already adopted or tested AI.
- `Confirmed`: The company wants to understand whether an AI idea is feasible before committing serious money.
- `Confirmed`: The buyer does not want to recruit, vet, manage, and later dissolve a temporary specialist team.
- `Inferred`: A failed internal attempt or lack of specialized expertise is likely a strong trigger, even if not always stated explicitly.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### Fears

- `Confirmed`: Spending money on something that never reaches production.
- `Confirmed`: Getting slideware or hand-wavy consulting instead of a working path forward.
- `Confirmed`: Being sold unrealistic promises on time, cost, or capability.
- `Inferred`: The site should directly answer “why won’t this become another expensive AI experiment?”

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

- `Inferred`: The website must do most of the commercial trust-building because sales capability is still immature.
