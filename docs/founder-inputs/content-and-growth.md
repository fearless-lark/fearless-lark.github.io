# Content and Growth

## Content Objective

- `Confirmed`: The founder wants content to build trust, demonstrate expertise, and create useful presence, not just chase visibility.
- `Confirmed`: The strongest content filter is value over noise.
- `Inferred`: Remilink’s content should make the company sound thoughtful and technically serious before it sounds prolific.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - сформировать идеи постов из голосовых заметок_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

| `Confirmed` | Product managers, business analysts, founders, CEOs, CTOs | Business/decision audience for strategy and risk-reduction content |

## Content Principles

- `Confirmed`: Avoid generic “AI LinkedIn” tone, recycled hype, and shallow trend commentary.
- `Confirmed`: Focus on expert interpretation, concrete lessons, and useful framing.
- `Confirmed`: Do not reveal more than is sensible at the current company stage.
- `Confirmed`: Long articles are acceptable only if they are genuinely strong; otherwise short, sharp posts are preferred.
- `Inferred`: Contrarian or skeptical takes will work best when they are grounded in direct delivery experience.

## Current Channel View

- `Confirmed`: LinkedIn is the primary channel.
- `Confirmed`: The founder’s own page should publish less frequently but at higher quality.
- `Exploratory`: Additional persona accounts may publish more frequently and target narrower audiences.
- `Inferred`: The website and LinkedIn should reinforce the same trust/value posture instead of feeling like separate brands.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Posting Cadence

- `Confirmed`: Founder account cadence is roughly once per week or once every two weeks.
- `Exploratory`: Persona accounts could publish around three times per week each.
- `Open`: Exact operating cadence, ownership, and editorial workflow are not yet defined.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Persona Strategy

- `Confirmed`: Any outreach/content personas should feel like real practitioners, not obvious sales avatars.
- `Confirmed`: Candidate persona types include a developer, project manager, business analyst, or domain expert.
- `Exploratory`: Persona backstories and documentation could live in the docs and become part of a broader content system.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Tooling and Workflow Ideas

- `Exploratory`: Use Notion as the operational board for content.
- `Exploratory`: Build a Telegram-to-LinkedIn pipeline that converts voice notes into draft posts.
- `Exploratory`: Host such a pipeline on Fly.io or another suitable platform.
- `Exploratory`: Use a small writing “swarm” or multi-role agent workflow for drafting, then require human refinement.
- `Exploratory`: Clay, Apollo, and LinkedIn Helper are current leadgen-tool ideas, not fixed commitments.

## Things to Avoid

- `Confirmed`: Empty frequency for its own sake.
- `Confirmed`: News reposting that target readers will already have seen elsewhere.
- `Confirmed`: Fake hype, inflated promises, and copy that sounds machine-generated even when a machine helped produce it.
