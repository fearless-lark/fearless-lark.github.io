# Delivery and Commercials

## Delivery Model

### Phase Overview

| Phase | Status | Current founder view |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | `Confirmed` | Clarify business problem, gather requirements, define scope, produce portable artifacts |
| POC | `Confirmed` | Validate the concept with minimal engineering and limited scope |
| MVP | `Confirmed` | Add integration, deployment readiness, and real engineering around the validated concept |
| Production | `Confirmed` | Scale, monitor, optimize, add automation and guardrails |

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Timeline and Pricing Snapshot

| Phase | Duration | Commercial shape | Notes |

| Free strategy call | ~2 hours | Free | Low-barrier first conversation |

## Discovery Deliverables

- `Confirmed`: Discovery and adjacent audit work can produce artifacts such as a PRD, technical blueprint, scope definition, and commercial proposal.
- `Confirmed`: These artifacts are portable; clients can use them with Remilink, another vendor, or an internal team.
- `Inferred`: Portability is a trust signal and should be emphasized carefully because it proves Remilink is not trying to trap the client.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Team Model

- `Confirmed`: A typical early-phase team includes the founder/architect, a business analyst, and selected developers; PM support is part-time in current estimates.
- `Confirmed`: The founder expects the BA role to be central because faster AI-native delivery increases the need for precise, non-contradictory specifications.
- `Confirmed`: Frontend exists as a capability but is not the core commercial offer.
- `Confirmed`: The founder wants small project staffing to be understood correctly: the client may pay for a team of 1–3 active people, but still benefits from the broader team’s collective AI expertise and architectural judgment.
- `Inferred`: Remilink should present itself as a real delivery team led by the founder, without pretending to be a giant agency.
- `Open`: Exact public wording for the current team model remains sensitive because the real staffing model is founder-led plus trusted collaborators.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

## Delivery Cadence

- `Confirmed`: The founder wants weekly sprint calls, regular demos, and weekly reporting so the client can see whether work is moving in the right direction.
- `Confirmed`: One differentiator is active budget protection during scoping: prove the concept on the smallest representative slice instead of multiplying redundant work.
- `Confirmed`: Good AI delivery depends on solid data-engineering practices underneath the model behavior, and Remilink wants that to be treated as part of the real implementation foundation.
- `Inferred`: The client experience should feel transparent, iterative, and founder-led.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Commercial Principles

- `Confirmed`: Discovery and audit are the clearest entry products.
- `Confirmed`: Larger build phases are primarily time-and-materials in the founder’s current thinking.
- `Confirmed`: The founder prefers honesty and transparency over over-promising, even though there is tension around scaring buyers with realistic timelines.
- `Confirmed`: AI-native delivery does not mean imposing a fixed internal tool stack on the client; actual tools and model choices should be aligned with enterprise restrictions, including on-prem requirements.
- `Exploratory`: Renaming phases or changing public wording to avoid scaring buyers was considered, but not decided.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Contact and Lead Handling

- `Confirmed`: The primary website action is contact-form submission, not calendar booking.
- `Confirmed`: Submissions should route to `info@remilink.com`.
- `Confirmed`: Response should be manual and happen within 24 hours.
- `Confirmed`: Replies should stay manual-only for now; no automated response is desired.
- `Confirmed`: No CRM is required yet at current expected lead volume.
- `Confirmed`: No calendar embed is desired by default.
- `Confirmed`: The founder is skeptical of calendar-first booking because buyers are unlikely to book those calls directly at first touch.
- `Inferred`: Contact copy should emphasize thoughtful review and value, not instant-response gimmicks.
- `Open`: The post-first-contact sales pipeline is still largely undefined.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

- `Confirmed`: Analytics is important for learning, but it is not a hard launch blocker if it lands immediately afterward.
- `Inferred`: The documentation should treat measurement as a near-term follow-up requirement, not a reason to delay launch forever.

## Commercial Risks to Keep Visible

- `Confirmed`: The company currently has more delivery confidence than sales maturity.
- `Confirmed`: Faster AI-native execution can shorten engagements and create utilization pressure unless the company also improves lead flow and conversion.
- `Exploratory`: Faster, cheaper “agentic POC” packaging may become a commercial wedge, but it should not be sold as a mature offer before the workflow exists.
