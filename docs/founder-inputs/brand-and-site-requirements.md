# Brand and Site Requirements

## The Site’s Job

- `Confirmed`: The website is expected to be the main sales conversion layer because sales operations are not yet mature.
- `Confirmed`: The site must make a qualified visitor want to keep reading within the first few seconds.
- `Inferred`: The site must reduce the amount of selling the founder has to do from cold on the first call.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Desired Impression Sequence

- `Confirmed`: First five seconds: the site looks visually clean, interesting, and credible enough to earn attention.
- `Confirmed`: Next five seconds: the content suggests there is something worth reading in detail.
- `Confirmed`: Third five seconds: the visitor begins to think “these may be the right people for the AI project we discussed.”

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Primary Action and Contact Flow

- `Confirmed`: The primary website CTA is form submission.
- `Confirmed`: The form should route to `info@remilink.com`.
- `Confirmed`: Follow-up should be manual within 24 hours.
- `Confirmed`: Replies should stay manual-only for now; no automated response sequence is desired.
- `Confirmed`: No calendar-first experience by default.
- `Exploratory`: A downloadable PDF describing Remilink’s approach could become a secondary CTA, but it is not yet committed.
- `Exploratory`: Showing the actual person who will reply, or a direct LinkedIn cue, may reduce form friction and is worth testing carefully.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/text/25.03.2026 remilink - competitors`

## Messaging and Tone

- `Confirmed`: Value and trust should dominate the site. Hype, pressure, and generic AI-marketing patterns should be avoided.
- `Confirmed`: The founder dislikes overused sales language such as artificial urgency and empty “we’ll answer in X hours” pressure copy.
- `Confirmed`: Leaving an inquiry should feel comfortable, low-pressure, and like the start of a helpful conversation rather than entry into a funnel.
- `Inferred`: The site should feel friendly, composed, and expert rather than aggressive or “look how many capabilities we have.”

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Brand Semantics

- `Confirmed`: The founder associates the `Remi` part of the brand with a “friend / helper” meaning and wants that undertone preserved.
- `Inferred`: The brand should come across as a capable guide who is glad to help, not as a distant or transactional consultancy.
- `Open`: Whether to explain this meaning explicitly on the public site remains unresolved.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

## Proof Rules

- `Confirmed`: The current site’s proof claims need audit before reuse.
- `Confirmed`: Anonymous case studies are acceptable, but they need more context and specificity.
- `Open`: Claims such as `100+ initiatives`, `top 0.001%`, `4.9/5 trust rating`, and exact speed/cost multipliers are not ready for public use without sources.
- `Inferred`: The site should lean on process honesty, detailed case narratives, founder credibility, and portable artifacts until proof claims are tightened.

Sources: `docs/Remilink_FounderBrief.md`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Founder and Team Presentation

- `Confirmed`: Professional founder photography is needed.
- `Confirmed`: A team photo or team-like visual presence is also desired because the site should not feel like a one-person consultancy, even though the real staffing model is lean.
- `Confirmed`: The founder wants the visual tone to be welcoming, not intimidating or sterile.
- `Open`: Exact team presentation language still needs care so the site remains honest without underselling delivery capability.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

## Site Structure Requirements

- `Confirmed`: The brandbook requires these core website sections: Services, Company, Contact Us, Blog, Case Studies, Industries.
- `Inferred`: A homepage plus these sections remains the right v1 structure, but “Company” may still be labeled “About” in implementation if needed.
- `Confirmed`: The site should stop trying to be “everything for everyone.”

Sources: `brandbook/website.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Visual Direction Inputs

- `Confirmed`: The written brandbook points to a human-modern, light, orange-accented direction.
- `Confirmed`: The image-heavy visual brandbook is useful as inspiration and reference material.
- `Inferred`: The visual `.docx` should be treated as a moodboard, not as a precise implementation system, because it is mostly mockups and image assets rather than a detailed rulebook.

Sources: `brandbook/brandbook_chatgpt_guide.docx`, `brandbook/brandbook_chatgpt_visuals.docx`

## Launch vs. Post-Launch Requirements

- `Confirmed`: Analytics matters and should be added to the site.
- `Confirmed`: Analytics is not required to block the first launch if it follows immediately afterward.
- `Confirmed`: Replacing `formsubmit.co` with a dedicated route to `info@remilink.com` is a higher-priority operational fix than analytics.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`
