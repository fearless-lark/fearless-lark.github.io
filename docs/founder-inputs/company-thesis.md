# Company Thesis

## What Remilink Is

- `Confirmed`: Remilink should be presented as an AI implementation partner that can diagnose the business problem, choose the right approach, validate it, and carry it through to MVP and production.
- `Confirmed`: The founder wants the company to feel helpful, trustworthy, calm, and human rather than loud, pushy, or hype-driven.
- `Inferred`: The strongest current story is not “we do every kind of AI for everyone,” but “we reduce risk by combining judgment, honest scoping, and delivery.”

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Why It Exists

- `Confirmed`: The founder’s origin story is rooted in long experience on both sides of outsourcing relationships: as the vendor and as the client-side evaluator.
- `Confirmed`: The company was started because the founder believes there is a better way to run AI projects for clients: more honest, more technically competent, and more aligned to business value.
- `Inferred`: The company narrative should highlight learned judgment and delivery maturity, not just technical skill.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## Positioning Pillars

### 1. Honest Expertise

- `Confirmed`: The founder wants Remilink to be explicit that not every problem needs the most fashionable AI solution.
- `Confirmed`: The company wants to be known for realistic estimates, transparent tradeoffs, and advice that protects the client from waste.
- `Inferred`: “We tell you what is worth building, what is not worth building yet, and how to validate the answer” is a stronger positioning frame than generic “AI transformation.”

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### 2. Validation Before Heavy Engineering

- `Confirmed`: The founder repeatedly describes a flow where Discovery clarifies the problem, POC proves the concept with minimal engineering, and only then do MVP and Production add heavier integration and operational work.
- `Confirmed`: A major differentiator is actively preventing clients from paying multiple times to prove the same thing.
- `Inferred`: “Validate before you scale” remains the best available strategic shorthand, even if exact hero copy is still open.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

### 3. AI-Native Delivery With Human Ownership

- `Confirmed`: Remilink wants to present itself as an AI-native company with internal workflows, strong models, and pipeline discipline.
- `Confirmed`: The founder is explicit that AI is used to speed up development, but every shipped output is still reviewed, validated, tested, and owned by humans.
- `Confirmed`: The founder wants Remilink to emphasize that strong data-engineering practice is part of the foundation of good AI delivery, not an afterthought.
- `Confirmed`: For clients with enterprise restrictions, Remilink aligns the actual tools and models used with the client’s constraints, including on-prem options where needed.
- `Open`: Public quantitative claims such as “30–40% faster” or “30–40% cheaper” need proof before they become website facts.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - AI native разработка_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

### 4. Founder-Led Credibility

- `Confirmed`: The founder is personally involved in every project as the chief architect and final decision-maker.
- `Confirmed`: Founder credibility should matter on the site because clients are buying judgment and trust, not a generic agency façade.
- `Inferred`: The company story should emphasize founder-led quality control rather than pretending the company is a large anonymous machine.
- `Confirmed`: Even when a project is staffed with only a few people, the founder wants clients to understand that they are getting the experience of a wider bench of engineers and architects specializing across AI disciplines.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`

## The AI-Native Outsourcing Thesis

- `Confirmed`: The founder believes AI will compress coding work and shift outsourcing value upward into specification quality, architecture, orchestration, testing, and business interpretation.
- `Confirmed`: In this worldview, the job of an outsourcing company becomes: structure the problem well, write better specs, choose the right technical direction, coordinate execution, and verify quality.
- `Confirmed`: The founder explicitly sees specification quality as multi-audience work now: the same documentation increasingly needs to serve business stakeholders, developers, and AI agents.
- `Exploratory`: The founder spends real time thinking about future “agent swarms,” BA tooling, and internal orchestration systems, but these are not yet sellable public offerings.
- `Inferred`: This future-facing thesis is strategically important, but the public website should use it as a credibility layer, not as the main promise unless the workflows are battle-tested.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - концепция аутсорсинг компании_ru.txt`

## Claims Discipline

- `Confirmed`: The founder wants to position the company as world-class in quality and unusually cost-efficient.
- `Open`: Claims such as “world-level quality,” “4–5x cheaper,” “50+ projects,” and speed/cost multipliers must be sourced or carefully reframed before public use.
- `Inferred`: The website should prefer specific case-study logic, portable artifacts, and honest methodology over unverified superlatives.

Sources: `docs/notes/raw/25.03.2026 remilink - специализация и стратегия_ru.txt`, `docs/notes/text/25.03.2026 remilink - competitors`

## What This Means for the Site

- `Confirmed`: The site should sell trust, judgment, and a sensible next step, not hype.
- `Confirmed`: It should quickly communicate that Remilink understands how to take an AI idea from ambiguity to validated scope and then into a real system.
- `Open`: Exact headline, tagline, and pricing-display posture remain unresolved until proof and claims are audited.
