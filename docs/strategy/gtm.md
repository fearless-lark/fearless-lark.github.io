## Website as Salesperson

The website must do disproportionate commercial work because the sales pipeline is still immature. Desired impression sequence:
1. **5 seconds**: Visually credible and worth reading
2. **10 seconds**: Content looks relevant
3. **15 seconds**: "These might be the right people for our AI project"

Success test: A VP-level visitor says to a colleague, "I found potentially good guys for implementing that AI project idea we were discussing."

## Primary CTA

**Button**: "Discuss Your AI Project"
**Subtext**: "Get an honest assessment of feasibility, timeline, and cost — directly from our chief architect."
**Founder photo** shown next to the form.

Why this wording:

| Considered | Problem |
|---|---|
| "Contact Us" | Too vague. Says nothing about what happens next. |
| "Get Started" | Too salesy. Implies commitment. |
| "Book a Free Strategy Session" | Only works for explorers. A CTO comparing vendors wants to describe their project, not get a "session." |
| "Estimate Project" | Commoditizes you. If 5 vendors get the same request, you're comparing spreadsheets, not expertise. |

"Discuss Your AI Project" works for all three visitor types: explorers (is this feasible?), buyers (here's what I need), researchers (give me something for my boss). The subtext puts "estimate" in the promise without making it the action.

Strategy session offer lives on the Services page, not as the main CTA.

## Contact Flow

- Form submissions → **info@remilink.com** via formsubmit.co (provides email hiding + DDoS protection — no replacement needed)
- Response: Founder personally within **24 hours**
- No calendar integration (founder believes nobody books calls through website calendars anymore)
- No automated responses — all manual
- No CRM needed yet (low volume expected)
- Form: Simple "What can we help with?" textarea. No dropdowns.

## Secondary CTA (PDF)

Deferred to post-launch. The methodology page URL serves as the shareable asset initially. Build PDF after 2–3 articles + 1–2 case studies exist. Ungated — optimize for trust, not email capture.

## Team Presentation

About page launches **founder-first** (photo available at `media/photos/founder/founder_001.jpg`). Team section shows **roles, not faces** (no team photos confirmed).

Language: "Every project is personally led by our founding architect. The team is assembled specifically for your challenge — drawing from specialists who've worked together across 50+ AI projects."

This is true: there ARE specialists, they ARE selected per project, the founder DOES lead everything. It doesn't claim a headcount or imply full-time staff.

Professional photoshoot: nice-to-have, not a blocker.
