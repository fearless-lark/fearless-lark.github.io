## The AI-Native Outsourcing Thesis

This is the founder's strategic worldview — important for understanding where the company is headed.

AI will compress coding work and shift outsourcing value upward into: specification quality, architecture, orchestration, testing, and business interpretation. The job of an outsourcing company becomes: structure the problem well, write better specs, choose the right technical direction, coordinate execution, and verify quality.

**The specification bottleneck**: "The next bottleneck will be exactly writing specifications and subsequent testing." The founder wonders if even this might be automated.

**Three-audience documentation**: A potential differentiator — documentation formats that serve developers, business people, AND AI agents simultaneously.

**The hype paradox**: Before, hype that everyone wanted AI drove demand. Now, hype that AI can build AI makes clients think they don't need outsourcing. The value shifts to expertise in orchestration, specification, architecture, and quality assurance.

This thesis is strategically important but should be used as a credibility layer on the site, not as the main promise — until the workflows are battle-tested.
