# Services

## Service Methodology

Framed as a journey on the site — not a menu. We move to each next phase only when the previous step is successfully secured.

| Public Label | What Happens | Deliverables | Duration | Pricing | Team |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Strategy Session** (free) | Review business ideas, examine tech stack, surface opportunities, identify what's NOT feasible. | Verbal feasibility assessment, opportunity mapping, recommendation on next steps. | 2 hours | Free | AI Architect |
| **AI Strategy Session** (paid) | Full-week comprehensive audit. Deep dive into business context, data landscape, technical readiness. | Written feasibility assessment with timelines, costs, quality expectations. Identification of non-feasible paths and data gaps. | 1 week | ~$5,000 | AI Architect, BA |
| **Discovery & Blueprint** | Meet stakeholders, gather requirements, create technical blueprint. Close collaboration with the client's business — we dive into all specifics of the business problem and technical nuances (infrastructure, data, etc.). We form the scope of the first iteration, making sure it's representative of the whole solution. | Development-ready specification for Concept Validation (PRD), high-level specification of the general solution vision, WBS, SOW, Technical Blueprint, Commercial Proposal. **Artifacts are portable** — client can take them anywhere. | 1–3 weeks | ~$5,000/week | 1 AI Architect FTE, Engineers PTE (DevOps, MLOps, ML Engineer, Backend Engineer, Data Engineer), 1 BA |
| **Concept Validation** | Validate AI concept with real data, no heavy integrations. Limited representative scope. | Working demo with real data. | 2–4 months | From $15,000/month | Architect (PT), 1-2 devs, 1 BA, 1 PM (PT) |
| **Production-Ready Build** | Integrate the validated solution into the client's systems, deploy, prepare for production. Real engineering begins. On this phase we add Software Engineers who start working on integrations and scalability alongside the ML Engineers — before that, all work is done only by MLEs, which saves the client's budget significantly. | Production-grade solution for subset of users/products. | 3–5 months | From $15,000/month | Same + SE, tester |
| **Scale & Optimize** | Full scaling, monitoring, guardrails, accuracy/speed optimization, production data pipelines. | Full production system with monitoring and guardrails. | 4–7 months | Varies (team scales) | Growing team |

**Agentic Concept Validation***: Available as an option alongside standard Concept Validation. Uses our agentic AI-driven workflows for approximately 30-40% faster/cheaper delivery. Recommended for projects where speed of validation is critical.

**Pricing model**: Fixed for Discovery/Audit. Can be fixed for Concept Validation. Primarily T&M for larger phases.

**Vendor management**: We are flexible and adjust to specific client requirements on every project. We handle LLM vendor management — subscriptions, workflows, cost of tooling. On-prem development without cloud AI is also an option. The client doesn't worry about vendor logistics.

All positioning pillars apply across every phase of the engagement — see [Positioning Pillars](positioning.md#positioning-pillars). Pricing display rules live in [gtm.md](gtm.md#pricing-display-rules).

## Appendix: Service Label Reasoning

| Internal Name | Public Label | Why This Name |
|---|---|---|
| AI Audit / Free Call | **AI Strategy Session** | "Audit" = looking for problems. "Strategy Session" = looking for opportunities. |
| Discovery | **Discovery & Blueprint** | "Blueprint" signals a tangible deliverable. "Discovery" alone = "we just talk." |
| POC | **Concept Validation** | Don't abbreviate. "Validation" = genuinely testing. |
| MVP | **Production-Ready Build** | "MVP" = startup connotation, "unfinished." This is real engineering + deployment. |
| Production | **Scale & Optimize** | Clear. All users, all products, monitoring, guardrails. |
