# Services

## Service Methodology

Framed as a journey on the site — not a menu.

| Public Label | What Happens | Duration | Pricing | Team |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Strategy Session** | Free 2h call or full-week audit. Review business ideas, examine tech stack, surface opportunities, identify what's NOT feasible. | 2h – 1 week | Free (2h) or ~$5,000 (week) | Founder/Architect, BA |
| **Discovery & Blueprint** | Meet stakeholders, gather requirements, create technical blueprint. Deliverables: VBS, PRD, Technical Blueprint, Commercial Proposal. **Artifacts are portable** — client can take them anywhere. | 1–3 weeks | $5,000–$10,000 | Architect, 1 dev, 1 BA |
| **Concept Validation** | Validate AI concept with real data, no heavy integrations. Demo with real data, limited scope. Prove one category, not ten. | 2–4 months | From $15,000/month | Architect (PT), 2 devs, 1 BA, 1 PM (PT) |
| **Production-Ready Build** | Integrate into main app, deploy, prepare for production. Real engineering begins. | 3–5 months | From $15,000/month | Same + tester |
| **Scale & Optimize** | Full scaling, monitoring, guardrails, accuracy/speed optimization, production data pipelines. | 4–7 months | Varies (team scales) | Growing team |

**Pricing model**: Fixed for Discovery/Audit. Can be fixed for Concept Validation. Primarily T&M for larger phases.

**Total lifecycle**: ~8–9 months ("like having a baby").

**Vendor management**: RemiLink handles LLM vendor management — subscriptions, workflows, cost of tooling. The client doesn't worry about it. On-prem: can guarantee all development stays on local infrastructure.

**Cross-cutting differentiators** (apply to all phases):
- Data engineering best practices as foundation
- Enterprise tooling flexibility (always align tools/models with client, including on-prem)
- Team expertise at individual prices (full bench behind every engagement)
- Weekly sprint calls, demos, and reports — "the client constantly knows what's happening"
- Active budget protection during scoping
- Post-project availability for questions

## Service Label Reasoning

| Internal Name | Public Label | Why This Name |
|---|---|---|
| AI Audit / Free Call | **AI Strategy Session** | "Audit" = looking for problems. "Strategy Session" = looking for opportunities. |
| Discovery | **Discovery & Blueprint** | "Blueprint" signals a tangible deliverable. "Discovery" alone = "we just talk." |
| POC | **Concept Validation** | Don't abbreviate. "Validation" = genuinely testing. "Confirmation" was rejected (implies rubber-stamping). |
| MVP | **Production-Ready Build** | "MVP" = startup connotation, "unfinished." This is real engineering + deployment. |
| Production | **Scale & Optimize** | Clear. All users, all products, monitoring, guardrails. |

## Pricing Display Rules

**Principle**: "Be transparent about the steps and the entry cost, let the conversation handle the rest."

- **Show on site**: Entry costs and monthly rates
- **Don't show**: Project totals or durations next to prices
- **Why**: "$15K/mo" feels manageable. "$60K total" invites comparison shopping. Same money, different psychology. Durations live in the methodology section, prices in the pricing section — never in the same table cell.

## Lead Vertical: AI Shopping Assistants for E-Commerce

The clearest concrete offering: intelligent search agents for complex product catalogs.
- Natural text queries against structured AND unstructured data (descriptions, reviews)
- Sales, consulting, and recommendation capabilities
- Target: specialized stores with thousands of products and complex navigation (auto parts, building materials, clothing, cosmetics)

## Secondary: Secure In-House AI Enablement

AI systems deployed on the client's own infrastructure for security-sensitive organizations:
- Task analysis, capacity estimation, cost assessment, setup assistance
- All data stays in-house
- Target: 50–1,000+ employees in financial, healthcare, insurance sectors

## Emerging: EdTech

Currently building a CRM for an online school. Possible AI use cases (tutoring agents, RAG for educational materials) identified but not yet developed. "We are not really experts in this sphere." Present carefully until proof is stronger.

## Shelved

- **Computer Vision**: Capability exists, deliberately shelved (niche market, hard to find clients)
- **CRM development**: No clear AI angle. Small companies can't afford it, big ones already have it.
- **Agentic POC variant**: 30–40% cheaper/faster POC using agentic approach. System doesn't fully exist yet — would need to be built during a project. Aspirational, not sellable today.
