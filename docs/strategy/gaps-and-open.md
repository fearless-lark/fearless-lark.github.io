# Gaps, Open Items & Claims Discipline

## Claims Discipline

Do NOT publish without sourced proof:
- "World-class quality" — needs specific evidence
- "4–5x cheaper" — needs benchmark
- "30–40% faster" — needs measurable reference
- "50+ projects" — must frame as founder career, not company
- ~~"100+ AI initiatives" / "4.9/5 rating" / "Top 0.001%"~~ — **KILLED**. Not carried to new site.

The company will look stronger by being specific and restrained than by stretching its proof.

- Building complex internal tooling or agent swarms

- Hiring a salesperson (can't brief them until positioning is battle-tested)
- Paid advertising (volume too low, positioning untested)
- Social media beyond LinkedIn
- Computer Vision as a service line
- Complex CRM integration
- Calendar-heavy booking flows
- Elaborate automation before basic content and inquiry systems work
