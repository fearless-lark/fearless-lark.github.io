# Domain Expertise Verticals

## Primary: AI Shopping Assistants for E-Commerce

Intelligent search and recommendation agents for online stores with complex product catalogs. The customer describes what they need in natural language — the AI searches across structured and unstructured data (specs, descriptions, reviews), recommends products, and assists through the buying process. For stores where thousands of products and complex navigation make traditional search fail.

## Secondary: Secure In-House AI Enablement

AI systems deployed on the client's own infrastructure for security-sensitive organizations:
- Task analysis, capacity estimation, cost assessment, setup assistance
- All data stays in-house
- Target: 50-1,000+ employees in financial, healthcare, insurance sectors

## Emerging: EdTech

Possible AI use cases identified but not yet developed.

### EdTech Pain Points (2025-2026)
1. **Post-pandemic funding pressure** — VC funding dropped ~89% from 2021 peak. Every tool must justify itself with measurable impact (retention, completion rates, revenue).
2. **Fragmented systems** — LMS, CRM, payments, analytics don't talk to each other. Manual data entry, broken student journeys, poor visibility.
3. **Low engagement and retention** — Course completion rates remain a core problem. One-size-fits-all content can't compete with personalized experiences.
4. **Administrative burden on instructors** — Grading, lesson planning, content creation consume hours that AI can reclaim.
5. **Data privacy compliance** — FERPA, state-level privacy laws, and new AI guidance create overhead smaller EdTech companies struggle with.

### Buildable AI Use Cases (by feasibility for our team)
- **RAG-powered tutoring/support agents** — Ground LLM in school's own course materials. Students get 24/7 curriculum-accurate answers. Most immediate opportunity — tools exist in market but most schools need custom implementations.
- **Automated content generation** — Quizzes, flashcards, lesson outlines, practice worksheets from source material. Same RAG infrastructure, different output.
- **Student analytics and at-risk prediction** — ML models tracking performance signals, predicting dropout. Uses data schools already collect but never analyze.
- **CRM-LMS integration with AI layer** — What we already do. Add AI for auto-enrollment, re-engagement triggers, learning behavior analysis.
- **Automated grading and assessment** — NLP-based essay grading, code evaluation. Mid-market schools lack access to tools that exist for higher-ed.

### Ideal EdTech Clients
- Online schools and e-learning platforms (100-1,000 employees)
- Companies with existing course content that could be enhanced with AI
- Organizations feeling competitive pressure from AI-native EdTech startups

### Honest Caveat
"We are not really experts in this sphere." The CRM project gives us a foothold, but we should lead with e-commerce where conviction is strongest and treat EdTech as a growth area where we build expertise through delivery.
