# Lead Generation

## Timeline

- **Now (week 1-2)**: Start Clutch profile, start LinkedIn content, launch website rebuild (inbound foundations)
- **Now (week 1-2)**: Start agency outreach + job board signals (outbound for fast results)
- **Month 2-3**: Inbound starts producing (SEO indexing, Clutch reviews, LinkedIn audience building)
- **Month 3+**: Inbound overtakes outbound as primary source

---

## Outbound Channels

### 1. Agency partnerships (highest leverage)

Find 10-20 Shopify Plus / Magento agencies and digital transformation firms. They have clients asking for AI capabilities they can't deliver. Become their AI partner.

- Approach: founder-to-founder outreach
- Revenue model: referral fee (10-15%) or white-label arrangement
- Volume: low outreach (20 agencies), but each agency = ongoing client pipeline
- Type: hybrid — you initiate, but leads arrive warm through the partner

### 2. Job board signal mining

Companies posting AI/ML roles = confirmed need + confirmed budget.

- Pitch: "I noticed you're hiring an ML Engineer for recommendations. We can deliver a production system in 8-10 weeks — faster than your hiring timeline."
- Sources: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, company career pages
- Tools: Apollo.io or Clay can filter by "companies currently hiring for [role]"

### 3. Apollo.io + Clay for enriched outbound (not LinkedIn Sales Navigator)

LinkedIn Sales Navigator has poor conversion for services at this price point. Use instead:

- **Apollo.io** (~$100/mo): Better company/contact data, verified emails, built-in sequences
- **Clay**: Enrichment engine — pulls recent news, tech stack, job postings, funding rounds per lead, auto-generates personalized first lines
- **Channel**: Cold email (8-15% response rate) over LinkedIn InMail (~3%)

## Inbound Channels

### 4. Clutch profile (slow build, high trust)

Free to list. After 2-3 client reviews, appears in "AI development company" searches. Worth starting now so it's producing by month 3-4.

### 5. E-commerce communities

eCommerceFuel (paid, serious 7-8 figure store owners), Shopify Partner community, Reddit r/ecommerce. Be genuinely helpful for 2-4 weeks before mentioning services.

### 6. SEO + GEO (long-term, compounding)

Not a first-customer channel — takes 3-6 months. But high-ticket B2B services have outsized ROI from organic: one organic lead/month = $180K+ ARR.

**SEO strategy:**
- Each page targets a keyword cluster (see table below)
- Case studies structured as searchable problem/solution pages, not marketing brochures
- Technical foundation: Astro = fast by default, JSON-LD Service + Organization schemas, semantic HTML
- Competition is low — most AI consultancies have terrible SEO

| Keyword cluster | Target page | Competition |
|---|---|---|
| "AI implementation partner e-commerce" | Homepage / Services | Low |
| "recommendation engine development" | Case study | Medium |
| "AI consulting for online retail" | Industries | Low |
| "build AI search for e-commerce" | Case study | Low |
| "AI POC to production" | Services (methodology) | Very low |

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):**

AI-savvy buyers (our ICP) increasingly use ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity for vendor research. What makes AI models recommend you:

- **llms.txt** — included in website v1 (trivial to implement, high signal)
- **Structured data** — JSON-LD Service schema on every service page, not just Organization on homepage
- **Third-party mentions** — Clutch profile, directory listings, partner site mentions (AI models weight these heavily)
- **Factual, specific content** — Our claims discipline approach naturally helps GEO. AI models prefer "we built a recommendation engine for a 50K SKU catalog" over "world-class AI solutions"
- **Answer real questions** — structure content to match queries like "who can build an AI recommendation engine for e-commerce"

### 7. LinkedIn organic content

See [smm.md](smm.md) — founder posts real AI implementation insights 2-3x/week. Not thought leadership fluff. DMs only to people who engage first (warm, not cold). Timeline: 6-8 weeks to build momentum. Makes all other channels work better.

## Three-Tier Outreach Model

| Tier | Target | Personalization | Time per lead | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dream 50** | 50 ideal companies | Full research, custom multi-touch | 30 min | Low |
| **Segment batches** | 500-1000, split by micro-segment | Segment template + 1-2 personal lines | 5 min | Main engine |
| **Content warming** | Everyone | LinkedIn posts + thoughtful comments | — | Background |

Micro-segments for Tier 2: "E-commerce with 10K+ SKUs," "Retailers posting AI job openings," "Companies on Shopify Plus / Magento Enterprise," "Companies with bad on-site search or recommendations."
