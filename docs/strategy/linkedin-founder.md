# Founder LinkedIn Profile — Audit & Optimization Plan

Profile: linkedin.com/in/oleh-yashchuk/

## Problem statement

The LinkedIn profile contradicts the website positioning. The website says "focused AI implementation for complex catalogs." The LinkedIn says "we do everything for everyone" (e-commerce, retail, manufacturing, edtech, healthcare, financial services, "and other data-rich businesses"). A CTO who visits remilink.com, likes it, then clicks through to the founder's LinkedIn sees a completely different story. That kills trust.

The profile must be aligned with the website positioning and optimized for one goal: a potential customer who lands on it either connects, visits remilink.com, or sends a message.

---

## 1. Headline

**Current**: "Senior AI Solution Architect @ AI Strategy, Deep Learning Solutions"

**Issues**:
- Doesn't mention RemiLink
- "Senior AI Solution Architect" undersells the founder role
- "@ AI Strategy, Deep Learning Solutions" is confusing — reads as random keywords, not a company name
- No value proposition for visitors
- No signal of target audience

**Recommended direction** (pick one, adapt):

- Option A: `Founder @ RemiLink | AI Search, Recommendations & Shopping Assistants for E-commerce | From strategy to production`
- Option B: `Founder @ RemiLink — We build AI for complex product catalogs | Architect-led, validation-first`
- Option C: `Founder @ RemiLink | Building AI search & recommendations for e-commerce companies with complex catalogs`

**Why**: The headline is the most-seen text on LinkedIn — it appears in search results, comments, connection requests, and messages. Every impression should reinforce what you do and for whom. "E-commerce" and "AI search" are the keywords buyers search for.

---

## 2. About section

**Current issues**:
1. "Everything for everyone" positioning — lists 7+ industries. Contradicts the website focus on e-commerce (primary), security-sensitive orgs (secondary), EdTech (emerging).
2. RemiLink is not named — "Today I lead an AI-focused engineering firm" misses a branding opportunity.
3. Wall of text — asterisk formatting doesn't render on LinkedIn. Shows as one unreadable paragraph.
4. Too many capability areas — LLM/RAG, forecasting, scoring, pricing, CV, signal processing. The website focuses on search, recommendations, and AI assistants.
5. No social proof — no numbers, no outcomes.
6. CTA is buried at the bottom of an unreadable block.

**Recommended structure**:

```
Paragraph 1 (2-3 sentences): Who you are + what RemiLink does + for whom
Paragraph 2 (3-4 arrow bullets): What clients get — specific, outcome-focused
Paragraph 3 (2-3 sentences): Why you're different — credibility, methodology
Paragraph 4 (1-2 sentences): CTA
```

**Draft**:

> I'm the founder and chief architect at RemiLink. We build AI search, recommendations, and shopping assistants for e-commerce companies with complex product catalogs.
>
> What that looks like in practice:
> → Intelligent search that understands natural language queries across thousands of SKUs
> → Recommendation engines that know product compatibility, not just "also bought"
> → AI assistants that guide customers through complex purchasing decisions
> → On-premise AI deployment for organizations with strict data policies
>
> Every project follows a validation-first approach — we prove the concept works with your real data before committing to full engineering. I personally architect every engagement, from first call through production deployment.
>
> Background: 10+ years in AI/ML. BSc Mathematics, MSc Statistics. Built AI solutions at SoftServe and multiple product companies before founding RemiLink.
>
> Considering an AI initiative? Send a message — I'll give you an honest assessment of what it would actually take.

## 10. Recommendations

**Current**: None visible.

**Impact**: Critical for a consulting/services business. A potential client wants to see that other people vouch for the founder.

**Action plan**:
1. Identify 3-5 people to ask: former clients, colleagues from SoftServe, collaborators from Spatially Health
2. Request they specifically mention: working together on AI projects, technical depth, honesty about feasibility, delivery quality
3. Even 2-3 strong recommendations dramatically change profile credibility
4. Offer to write recommendations for them in return (common LinkedIn practice)
