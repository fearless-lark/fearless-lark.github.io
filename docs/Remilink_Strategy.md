# Remilink Strategic Positioning

## Context

Remilink has broad AI capabilities (classical ML, CV, NLP, audio/DSP, LLMs, agents, RAG, MLOps) across multiple domains (finance, healthcare, ecommerce, manufacturing, edtech). The challenge: how to specialize in a saturated market where every company claims to "do AI."

Two key truths:

1. **Remilink's real strength is the full lifecycle** — from first hearing a business problem, through consulting/scoping, discovery with stakeholders, PoC (AI-only, no integrations), MVP (scaled with integrations), to production. The same team consults AND builds. No handoff, no lost context.

2. **The team is technically excellent but commercially junior** — the quality of work is high, but the ability to frame, present, and sell it lags behind. The website must compensate for this gap.

---

## The Core Problem

"We can do everything" is the worst positioning in a crowded market:

- LLM wrappers are a race to the bottom — every dev shop claims "we build AI agents"
- The big labs keep eating the middleware — what required a team 12 months ago is a weekend project now
- Enterprise buyers are smarter after 2 years of AI hype — they want someone who understands their problem
- Generalist = commodity pricing — specialists command premium rates because they reduce buyer risk

---

## What's Actually Scarce

The market is drowning in people who can call an API. What's hard to find:

1. **The full lifecycle in one team** — most shops either consult (slides) or build (to spec). Very few diagnose the problem, design the solution, validate it, AND build it to production.

2. **Classical ML + modern AI combined** — most new shops are LLM-only. A team that can honestly say "you don't need an LLM for this" earns massive trust.

3. **Production deployment expertise** — the gap between notebook and production is enormous. Most shops deliver POCs. Very few deliver production systems.

4. **Honest scoping** — the PRD with in-scope / out-of-scope, the PoC-first approach, the "validate before you scale" methodology. This is risk reduction, and it's what enterprise buyers actually want.

---

## Positioning Direction

### Two colors only

**Color 1: Risk reduction** — "We prove it before you scale" (your investment is safe)
**Color 2: Honest expertise** — "We know what actually works" (trust our judgment)

Everything on the site serves one of these two messages. Nothing else.

### Working hero

**Headline:** "Validate fast, scale smart."
**Support:** "We prove AI works for your problem before you commit serious budget."
**CTA:** "Book a Discovery Call"

## Services — Reframed as a Journey

Frame services as engagement stages, not capability categories:

### 1. Discovery & Solution Design
Diagnose the business problem, assess what AI approaches fit, engage stakeholders, deliver a technical blueprint with clear scope, deliverables, and projections.

### 2. Proof of Concept (2-4 months)
Focused AI development. No unnecessary integrations. Real results before committing further budget.

### 3. MVP & Scale
Validated concept gets integrated, hardened, and scaled. Backend, frontend, operations. Constant delivery, constant communication.

### 4. Production Operations
Monitoring, optimization, retraining. The system keeps working after launch.

## Industry Strategy

- **Lead with:** E-commerce and EdTech (strongest proof)
- **Secondary:** Manufacturing, Healthcare (some experience)
- **Drop from v1:** Oil & Gas, Logistics, Professional Services, Financial Services (unless real proof exists)

Industries are proof points, not the positioning itself.

## The Website as Salesperson

The website must do the commercial heavy lifting:
- A qualified buyer arrives at the discovery call already understanding Remilink
- The call becomes about their problem — a conversation the team is already great at
- Content marketing (2 articles/month, LinkedIn) replaces outbound sales
