# Remilink Strategic Positioning

> Updated 25.03.2026 after processing founder voice recordings. For the full structured extraction, see [`Remilink_FounderNotes.md`](Remilink_FounderNotes.md). For question-by-question answers, see [`Remilink_FounderBrief_Answered.md`](Remilink_FounderBrief_Answered.md).

## Current Strategic Reading

Remilink should not position itself as a generic AI shop that can do everything for everyone. The strongest current story is narrower and more believable:

- Founder-led AI implementation with 11 years of experience
- Validation before heavy engineering ("prove it before you scale")
- Honest scoping and budget protection
- AI-native delivery with full human ownership of code quality
- Full-lifecycle support: Discovery → POC → MVP → Production
- World-class quality at 4–5x lower cost than top outsourcers

The commercial challenge is not technical capability. It is trust, proof, and market clarity. The founder states it directly: "Doing great and selling great are two different classes."

---

## Positioning Pillars

### 1. Honest Expertise
Remilink sounds like a company that knows what NOT to build, when NOT to overuse LLMs, and how to keep the client from wasting money. The founder's anti-hype philosophy: "Value and trust, not hype."

### 2. Validation Before Scale
The company's strongest message: prove the concept before the client commits to full engineering and operations. This is risk reduction — the #1 concern for enterprise buyers.

**Working hero headline**: "Validate fast, scale smart."
**Support**: "We prove AI works for your problem before you commit serious budget."

### 3. AI-Native, Human-Owned Delivery
RemiLink uses proprietary AI-driven workflows that enable 30–40% faster development. But every piece of code is reviewed, validated, and tested by humans. The client doesn't pay extra for this — they save money.

**Enterprise tooling flexibility**: For enterprise clients with restrictions on specific coding tools or AI models, RemiLink always aligns the exact toolset and model stack with the client — including fully on-prem instruments where required. AI-native doesn't mean "our way or the highway."

### 4. Data Engineering Done Right
AI solutions are only as good as the data pipelines underneath them. RemiLink applies best practices in data engineering as the foundation for every AI project — something many competitors skip or do poorly. This is a quiet but critical differentiator.

### 5. Team Expertise at Individual Prices
Even on projects staffed with 1–2–3 people, the client gets access to the experience and expertise of the entire RemiLink team — engineers and architects specializing across all AI disciplines. In practice, you're paying for a small team but getting the collective judgment of a full bench of senior experts.

### 6. Founder-Led Credibility
This is a trust-led B2B service. The founder (BSc pure mathematics, MSc theoretical/applied statistics, 11 years in AI/ML) is personally involved in every project as chief architect and final decision-maker.

## What Remilink Actually Sells

### 1. AI Strategy Session & Discovery (Entry Point)

The lowest-friction entry point is diagnosis:

- **AI Strategy Session**: Free 2-hour call (no commitment) or full week (~$5,000)
- **Discovery & Blueprint**: 1–3 weeks, $5,000–$10,000
- Deliverables are portable — client can take them to any other company

Strategically important because it matches the founder's strongest differentiator: judgment.

### 2. Validation-First AI Delivery (Core Offering)

**Public-facing service labels** *(decided 26.03.2026)*:

| Internal Name | Public Label | Why This Name |
|---|---|---|
| AI Audit / Free Call | **AI Strategy Session** | "Audit" = looking for problems. "Strategy Session" = looking for opportunities. |
| Discovery | **Discovery & Blueprint** | "Blueprint" signals a tangible deliverable. "Discovery" alone = "we just talk." |
| POC | **Concept Validation** | Don't abbreviate. "Validation" = genuinely testing. "Confirmation" was rejected (implies rubber-stamping). |
| MVP | **Production-Ready Build** | "MVP" = startup connotation, "unfinished." This is real engineering + deployment. |
| Production | **Scale & Optimize** | Clear. All users, all products, monitoring, guardrails. |

Framed as a journey on the site: AI Strategy Session → Discovery & Blueprint → Concept Validation → Production-Ready Build → Scale & Optimize. This differentiates from competitors who show no methodology at all.

**Durations** (shown on methodology section, separate from pricing):

| Phase | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Discovery & Blueprint | 1–3 weeks |
| Concept Validation | 2–4 months |
| Production-Ready Build | 3–5 months |
| Scale & Optimize | 4–7 months |

**Pricing** *(corrected 26.03.2026)*: Entry from $5K (Discovery). Development phases from $15,000–18,000/month. Fixed for Discovery/Audit, T&M for larger phases.

**Pricing display principle**: Show entry costs and monthly rates on the site. Don't show totals. Don't show durations next to prices (prevents mental math to scary totals — "$15K/mo × 4 months" is psychologically different from "$60K"). Durations live in the methodology section. Principle: "Be transparent about the steps and the entry cost, let the conversation handle the rest."

Key principle: "If we need to prove search works in a category, we do it for ONE category, not ten."

### 3. AI Shopping Assistants for E-Commerce (Lead Vertical)

The clearest concrete offering: intelligent search agents for complex product catalogs.
- Natural text queries considering structured data AND unstructured data (descriptions, reviews)
- Sales, consulting, and recommendation capabilities
- Target: specialized stores with thousands of products and complex navigation (auto parts, building materials, etc.)

### 4. Secure In-House AI Enablement (Secondary)

AI systems deployed on the client's own infrastructure for security-sensitive organizations:
- Task analysis, capacity estimation, cost assessment, setup assistance
- All data stays in-house
- Target: 50–1,000+ employees in financial, healthcare, insurance sectors

### 5. EdTech (Cautious / Emerging)

Currently building a CRM for an online school. Possible AI use cases (tutoring agents, RAG for educational materials) identified but not yet developed. The founder acknowledges: "We are not really experts in this sphere." Present carefully until proof is stronger.

## ICP Direction

### Primary
- **Mid-sized and larger companies with complex product catalogs and/or real operational complexity**
- Why complexity over headcount: A 150-person auto parts retailer with 50,000 SKUs is a better fit than a 5,000-person company selling 3 products. Company size is a proxy; complexity is the real qualifier. The "and/or" is deliberate — e-commerce clients have catalog complexity, in-house AI clients have operational complexity, some have both.
- Specialized e-commerce businesses with large/complex catalogs
- Buyers: CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Product Owners, founders
- Ideal: companies where competitors have already adopted AI

### Secondary
- Organizations with strict data-security requirements needing in-house AI
- Financial, healthcare, insurance companies
- Buyers: CTOs, CEOs

### Emerging
- EdTech (needs proof and packaging)

### Not Strategic Truth (Yet)
- Broad multi-industry specialization
- Manufacturing, healthcare, or financial services as named verticals (unless real proof surfaces)
- Any claim about deep vertical authority where current proof is thin

## Go-to-Market

### Channel: LinkedIn (Primary)
- **Founder's personal page**: High-quality posts, once per week or every two weeks
- **Persona accounts**: 3 posts/week each; must feel like real people (developer, PM, BA, domain expert), not salespeople
- **Outreach**: Contact database building (tools under evaluation), automated messaging
- **Content philosophy**: Value and trust, not frequency. "We are not news channels."

### Website as Salesperson
The website must do disproportionate commercial work because the sales pipeline is still immature. It must:
- Explain what Remilink does in the first 5 seconds
- Hook the visitor in the next 5 seconds
- Make them want to study the site in detail by the 15-second mark
- Show a sensible, low-pressure next step (contact form → info@remilink.com)
- Demonstrate judgment, not just capability volume

The founder's success test: a VP-level visitor says to a colleague, "I found potentially good guys for implementing that AI project idea we were discussing."

### Primary CTA *(decided 26.03.2026)*
- **Button**: "Discuss Your AI Project"
- **Subtext**: "Get an honest assessment of feasibility, timeline, and cost — directly from our chief architect."
- Founder photo shown next to the form
- Strategy session offer lives on Services page, not as the main CTA

**Why this wording**: "Contact Us" is too vague. "Book a Free Strategy Session" only works for explorers who don't know what they need — a CTO comparing vendors wants to describe their project, not get a "session." "Estimate Project" commoditizes you — if 5 vendors get the same request, you're competing on spreadsheets, not expertise. "Discuss Your AI Project" works for all three visitor types: explorers (is this feasible?), buyers (here's what I need), and researchers (give me something for my boss). The subtext puts "estimate" in the promise without making it the action.

### Team Presentation *(decided 26.03.2026)*
- About page launches **founder-first** (photo available at `media/photos/founder/founder_001.jpg`)
- Team section shows **roles, not faces** (no team photos confirmed yet)
- "Every project is personally led by our founding architect. The team is assembled specifically for your challenge — drawing from specialists who've worked together across 50+ AI projects."
- Professional photoshoot: nice-to-have, not a blocker

**Why founder-first**: This IS a founder-led company — the founder architects every project. Leading with him is honest and differentiating. Enterprise buyers care more about the person who'll architect their system than about a grid of headshots. The role-based team section ("AI Architects · ML Engineers · Data Engineers · Business Analysts") conveys capability without requiring photos or implying a fixed headcount. Avoids: specific headcount claims, org charts, "Our dedicated team is always ready" language. When team photos become available, add them — the page works without them.

1. AI Strategy Session (free 2h or $5K full week)
2. Discovery & Blueprint ($5,000–$10,000)
3. Concept Validation (from $15K/mo)

Currently nonexistent — this is a critical gap that must be built.

## Competitive Differentiation

Based on analysis of 4 competitors (coxit.co, shelpuk.com, blackthorn.ai, parslabs.org):

**Common competitor problems** that RemiLink must avoid:
- Overloaded homepages (shelpuk.com — 20–40 seconds to read main page)
- Generic positioning without domain focus (coxit.co — "Dedicated Engineers & Custom AI/ML Development")
- Overdesigned elements that interfere with content (blackthorn.ai)
- Looking like a product company instead of outsourcing (parslabs.org)
- No visible methodology (blackthorn.ai — no Discovery/POC/MVP/Production)

**RemiLink's differentiation**:
1. AI specialization (not engineers trying to do AI)
2. Clear standardized methodology (Discovery & Blueprint → Concept Validation → Production-Ready Build → Scale & Optimize)
3. Transparency in estimates, timelines, and process
4. AI-native workflows for development speed — with enterprise tooling flexibility (always align tools/models with client, including on-prem)
5. Full code ownership and human verification
6. Consulting capability alongside delivery
7. Data engineering done right — best practices as foundation, not an afterthought
8. Team expertise at individual prices — even 1–2 person projects get the full team's collective experience
9. World-class quality at 3–5x lower cost

## The Agentic POC Variant

A potential near-term offering: an "agentic-based approach" where:
- POC is 30–40% cheaper and faster (1–2 months instead of 2–4)
- Deliberately for POC only (not production), with acceptable quality caveats
- Detailed Discovery still required before any POC
- **Status**: The agentic development system doesn't fully exist yet — this is aspirational. The founder proposes building it on the UTR project as a proof point.

## Claims Discipline

Do not turn the following into settled website copy without sourced proof:
- "World-class quality" — needs specific evidence
- "4–5x cheaper" — needs benchmark or comparison methodology
- "30–40% faster" — needs measurable reference
- "50+ projects" — must be clearly framed as founder career proof, not company proof
- ~~"100+ AI initiatives" / "4.9/5 rating" / "Top 0.001%"~~ — **KILLED (26.03.2026)**. Not carried to new site.
- Ratings, rankings, or trust scores without named sources

The company will look stronger by being specific and restrained than by stretching its proof.
